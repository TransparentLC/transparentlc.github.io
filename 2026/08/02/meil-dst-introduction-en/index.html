Documentation for Original Character Mod Mei.L for Don't Starve Together
Mj
2026-08-02 16:47:22

This translation is based on the original Chinese documentation. Some in-game text has not yet been localized; we are working to complete it as quickly as possible. Thank you for your patience, and we apologize for any inconvenience.

Thank you for playing Mei.L! If you encounter a bug, please leave a comment on the Workshop page with details or steps to reproduce it. Feedback and suggestions are also welcome.

The mod is now available! Download it from the Steam Workshop::Otherworldly Deity — Mei.L.

Introduction

Before reading the detailed documentation, we recommend skimming the Workshop description for an overview of Mei.L’s main abilities.
Before your first playthrough, please also read through the available mod configuration options.

A god…dess from another world?

  • Her powers seem to be only partially unleashed. She draws strength from Faith.
  • A benevolent deity, or a malevolent one?
  • Hey, don’t cry! All right, all right, I’ll believe in you!

Survival Odds: Someone help me out of this easy-mode wheelchair!

Name: Mei.L

Press O by default to open the status panel. This key can be changed in the settings.

The status panel displays level-up requirements, ability cooldowns, and current Sin, shown either as a tier or an exact value depending on the settings. Faith has its own persistent display.
The number in parentheses beside Sin shows how much Sin is expected to be added at the start of the next day under the current conditions.

Most abilities have a dedicated character line to announce that their cooldown has ended.

In Goddess Form, the status panel displays the 15% Health threshold, rounded up.

Base Stats

Starting stats: Hunger 75, Sanity 250, Health 75
Damage multiplier: 0.5

Stats at maximum level (Lv. 40): Hunger 300, Sanity 400, Health 350
Damage multiplier: 1.75

Leveling up requires progress in two separate meters: Experience and Conviction. At every 10-level milestone, Mei.L must also submit offerings at the Shrine to advance. The required offerings are shown when interacting with the Shrine in-game.

If one meter has met the current level’s requirement but another has not, the completed meter can continue accumulating progress. The stored amount cannot exceed the requirement for the current level plus 5.

Experience:
Gain Experience when a hostile creature dies nearby, within approximately 2.5× a Lantern’s light radius. Here, hostile creatures are those whose deaths do not increase Sin, excluding Werepigs. Throughout this document, 1 LR means 1 Lantern light radius. Experience gained = creature Health ×1%.
The creature only needs to die within range. It does not need to be targeting Mei.L, and Mei.L does not need to have damaged it.

Conviction:
Unless otherwise stated, any action that grants Faith also grants the same amount of Conviction.

Offerings:
Lv. 10: Meat ×10, Cut Reeds ×5, Nightmare Fuel ×3
Lv. 20: Moon Shards ×10, Moon Rocks ×5, Purple Gem ×1
Lv. 30: Thulecite ×10, Thulecite Fragments ×1, Pure Horror ×1
Lv. 40: Enlightened Crown ×1, Shadow Thurible ×1, Moon Rocks ×20

Faith

In the descriptions below, allies means other players and all creatures that are currently befriended, including Pig Men, Bunnymen, and Rock Lobsters. Buffs that affect allies also affect Mei.L herself.
Mei.L has a Faith stat with a fixed maximum of 500. This maximum cannot be changed by any means.

Faith changes under the following conditions:

  • Lose a fixed 50 Faith at the start of each new day.
  • Gain 20 Faith when befriending a Pig Man, Bunnyman, or another recruitable creature.
  • Gain 25 Faith per befriended creature every 30 seconds while it continues following Mei.L.
  • Lose 30 Faith each time Mei.L attacks a currently befriended creature.
  • Feeding another player grants Faith equal to the food’s Health-restoration value ×5.
  • When another player dies, lose 50% of current Faith, rounded up.
  • Killing a Boss grants Faith equal to enemy Health ×0.5% × (number of befriended Bunnymen + befriended Pig Men + allies).
  • Crafting a particular exclusive recipe for the first time grants 50 Faith.
  • Killing a neutral creature costs 10 Faith.
  • For sources tied to items, structures, and recipes, see the relevant item descriptions.
  • Slaying a Boss grants a buff that restores 20 Faith per second for one day. This regeneration does not grant Conviction.

The colored fill in the in-game stained-glass display represents current Faith.
A character line appears when Faith reaches zero. At that point, passive abilities stop working and Mei.L’s current followers leave; traits outside the Passive Abilities sections remain active.

Sin

Mei.L has a Sin stat with a fixed maximum of 3000. Its exact value is hidden by default, but can be enabled in the settings. The maximum cannot be changed by any means. Mei.L starts with 1000 Sin when entering the world or being resurrected.

Sin is gained in the following ways:

  • Killing a neutral or passive creature adds its Health as Sin. Until an action reduces Sin, each such kill increases the Sin gained from the next kill through this source by 10%.
  • When the exclusive item Knife kills a hostile creature, half the excess damage from the killing blow is added as Sin. This does not apply to area-of-effect damage.
  • Eating with excess Hunger restoration grants Sin equal to the excess ×2.
  • Eating with excess Health restoration grants Sin equal to the excess ×5.
  • Eating with excess Sanity restoration grants Sin equal to the excess ×1.
  • Going a full game day without crafting or cooking grants 200 Sin. For each additional consecutive day without crafting, the amount doubles. Reaching maximum Sin resets this escalation.
  • Going a full game day without attacking hostile creatures or gathering resources, such as chopping trees, mining rocks, picking grass, or harvesting bananas, grants 500 Sin. Each additional consecutive day in this state doubles the amount. This penalty does not apply while on a boat in the Mid Ocean or Deep Ocean. Reaching maximum Sin resets this escalation.
  • Stealing a Tallbird Egg grants 300 Sin.

Sin is reduced in the following ways:

  • Leaving Demon God Form for any reason immediately clears all Sin.
  • Killing a Boss grants Sin reduction of 5 per second for 4 minutes. Killing multiple Bosses can stack the reduction rate. Treeguards and Spider Queens do not count as Bosses for this effect.
  • Planting, tending, watering, fertilizing, and harvesting reduce Sin by 30/10/200/200/100 respectively, per action.
  • Crafting or cooking any item reduces Sin by 200 and resets the escalation of the no-crafting/no-cooking penalty.
  • Each action listed under the ‘full game day without attacking hostile creatures or gathering resources’ source reduces Sin by 10 and resets the escalation of that inactivity penalty.

Exclusive items may have additional ways to affect Sin, although none are currently implemented in the items covered here.

Sin has the following global effects on Mei.L:

  • At 0 Sin:
    • Birds land around Mei.L more frequently, approximately twice as often.
    • The chance of producing Seeds is higher, approximately doubled.
    • Crafting an item from the Refine category has a 25% chance to produce a bonus yield.
    • Tallbirds do not attack Mei.L.
  • At 0–1000 Sin:
    • Mei.L does not startle neutral creatures such as birds, Rabbits, or Butterflies.
    • Tending a crop automatically tends all crops within 1 LR.
    • When Mei.L is caught in darkness, she is immune to Charlie’s first attack.
  • Above 1000 Sin: Each point above 1000 increases damage taken by 0.05%.
  • Above 1500 Sin:
    • Within 1 LR of Mei.L, birds stop landing, Rabbits stay in their holes, and Flowers stop spawning Butterflies. Passive creatures actively try to move away from her whenever possible.
    • Her protection against Merm and Bunnyman aggression no longer applies. Bunnymen treat her as though she were carrying meat, whether or not she actually is.
  • Above 2000 Sin:
    • Neutral creatures capable of attacking become hostile toward Mei.L. Spiders actively move away from her, and she cannot lure them out by walking on their webs.
    • Chopping trees increases the chance of spawning a Treeguard by twice the base chance.
    • Crafting and gathering speed are reduced to 1/2.
  • Above 2500 Sin: Entering any condition in which Charlie can attack, usually complete darkness, immediately triggers two attacks from Charlie. This is what ‘losing visibility’ means here.
  • At 3000 Sin:
    • Once triggered, this state persists until Sin falls below 2500, or Mei.L leaves Demon God Form.
    • Hounds and Shadow Creatures spawn immediately, then every 2 minutes, with permanent aggression toward Mei.L. Enemies spawned this way have no drops.
    • Movement speed increases by 200%, and the damage multiplier gains a separate multiplicative 25% bonus. Mei.L continuously loses 2 Health per second, but becomes immune to all negative effects of Wetness, temperature, Sanity, Enlightenment, and Hunger.
    • These enemies do not switch targets unless attacked by another player. They disappear if Mei.L dies.
    • Butterflies personally killed by Mei.L are guaranteed to drop Butter.

Black hair on the character in front of the stained glass indicates that Sin is above 2000. At maximum Sin, the window shatters.
Dedicated character lines appear when Sin reaches 0, exceeds 2000, or reaches its maximum.

Traits Shared by All Forms

  • Insight:
    • Permanently unlocks one random technology recipe every 5 game days, becoming every 3 days after Lv. 20 and every 2 days after Lv. 30. Sculpture recipes, Cartography recipes, and other characters’ exclusive recipes are excluded.
    • Recipes normally available only near special crafting stations, such as Lunar Island or Ancient recipes, remain permanently available after being unlocked. The original station is no longer required.
    • Manually crafting one of these recipes also makes it permanently available after its first craft.
  • Absolute Sanity:
    • Sanity is unaffected by any continuous positive or negative aura. It can still be changed by discrete effects such as spellcasting, eating, and the Nightmare Amulet. Sanity effects from Mei.L’s own skills normally take priority over Absolute Sanity, so Divine Might’s continuous drain still applies.
    • While wearing the Enlightened Crown, Mei.L is treated as sufficiently sane at any Sanity level, and its Minor Gestalt attacks do not consume her Sanity.
    • She is immune to the Enlightenment effect of 5 Celestial Jewels.
  • Sovereignty:
    • Mei.L does not bow to the Bee Queen Crown.
    • She is immune to the monkey curse.
    • She is immune to forced unequipping, inventory removal, theft, items being knocked out of her possession, electrocution, and slipping on ice.
    • She takes no harvesting damage from plants such as Spiky Bushes, Roses, and Cacti.
    • She can always tell the current time while in the Caves.
    • She does not produce a Deadelgänger.
    • Under the default configuration, she cannot be grabbed by Ickers.

Form Mechanics

Traits not listed under Passive Abilities remain active when Faith reaches zero. Sanity and Enlightenment are two states of the same underlying stat; switching between them normally preserves its value unless a specific effect changes or resets it. Entering Demon God Form explicitly resets this value, as described below.
By default, each form’s active abilities use Z X C V, in the order listed. These bindings can be changed in the settings.

Note that C is also bound to crafting the previous recipe by default in the game.

All flight abilities grant +100% movement speed while active. This can be adjusted in the mod configuration.

Base Form

When Faith is above 300, Goddess Form and Demon God Form also gain Base Form’s Divine Might and Divine Domain passives.

Passive Abilities:

  • Divine Might (unlocked at Lv. 10):
    • Mei.L is invulnerable to damage while fewer than 3 enemies are targeting her. This does not work against Bosses.
    • While this protection is active because enemies are present, she loses 1 Sanity per second.
    • If the number of enemies increases and the visual effect disappears, the protection persists for 1 additional second, giving time to react.
  • Divine Domain (unlocked at Lv. 20):
    • Above 100 Faith, Mei.L gains immunity to knockback. Hostile summons created within 1 LR are immediately killed, including Bee Queen’s and Dragonfly’s minions and Ancient Fuelweaver’s crawling minions; enemies that have already spawned are unaffected when they enter the area. Fires and Deck Illuminators within the area do not consume their remaining burn time, food on the ground does not lose freshness, and smoldering and lightning strikes are prevented. Mei.L is treated as being in a dry environment and as a generator with unlimited power.
    • Above 200 Faith, the temperature in a very small area around Mei.L, roughly close enough for another player standing right beside her, is locked at 25°C. Food carried by Mei.L regains freshness, and characters within the area are immune to Moonstorms, Acid Rain, Miasma, and Sandstorms.
    • Divine Domain has a visible range indicator. Press H by default to hide it; this binding can be changed in the settings.
  • Divine Authority (unlocked at Lv. 40):
    • When Mei.L uses Divine Un…leashing? at maximum Faith, entering Goddess Form immediately clears the weather, melts winter snow, and fully restores all allies’ Health, Hunger, and Sanity, including Mei.L’s own. Those allies cannot lose Health for the next 30 seconds. Entering Demon God Form immediately turns the weather into a thunderstorm and freezes enemies within 1 LR in time until Mei.L makes her first attack or they lose aggro. For the next 30 seconds, her attacks consume Sanity equal to 1% of their damage.

Active Abilities:

  • Divine Un…leashing?: Reduces Faith to 1, fully unleashes her divine powers, grants immunity to the Faith loss at the start of a new day, and transforms Mei.L into Goddess Form or Demon God Form. See those forms for details. Cannot be used at 100 Faith or less.
  • My First Wife’s… Scythe? (unlocked at Lv. 15): Performs a wide slash around Mei.L, covering 1.5 LR. Enemies in range take 3 hits, each dealing 1× the held weapon’s attack damage. Costs 50 Faith.
  • My Second Wife’s… Pocket Watch? (unlocked at Lv. 25): Produces a pocket watch from another dimension, stopping all actions of nearby creatures within 5 LR, excluding players, for 10 seconds. Costs 30 Faith. Cooldown: 1 minute.

Goddess Form

Enter this form by using Divine Un…leashing? in Base Form with less than 2000 Sin, or by using A Devil’s Kindness in Demon God Form.
Reaching maximum Sin in Goddess Form immediately transforms Mei.L into Demon God Form.
Below 50% Health, Mei.L gains flight.
Below 15% Health, she returns to Base Form and gains 5 seconds of invulnerability.
Health cannot be restored by any means in Goddess Form. Any Health restoration from food is treated as excess healing for Sin calculations.
Provides light with the same range as a Lantern.
In Goddess Form, Mei.L is immune to damage from fire, Moonstorms, Acid Rain, and Miasma.
Sources of natural Sin accumulation are disabled in Goddess Form.

Passive Abilities:

  • Heart of Mercy: Grants 99.9% damage reduction, but all damage dealt by Mei.L is forced to 1 point of true damage.
  • Heart of the Healer (unlocked at Lv. 5):
    • When enemies damage allies within 20 turf tiles, that damage is redirected to Mei.L.
    • Continuously applies fertilization and Ice Flingomatic effects within 1 LR, or advances crops to their next growth stage. Each action performed by this effect costs 1 Health.
  • Heart of Salvation (unlocked at Lv. 30): Grants immunity to sleep, hitstun, and knockback. Above 100 Faith, Hunger drains at 1/3 of its normal rate.

Active Abilities:

  • Angel’s Wings (unlocked at Lv. 10):
    • Costs 20 Health. Mei.L spreads her wings to protect her companions, granting all allies, including herself, +0.5 to their damage multiplier and +15% movement speed. Non-player allies also gain 30% damage reduction. Duration: 30 seconds. Cooldown: 1 minute.
    • Grants flight for the duration, even above half Health.
  • Wrath of Mercy (unlocked at Lv. 20): Costs 100 Health. Calls down lightning on nearby enemies and player ghosts, dealing 100 damage and resurrecting player ghosts.

Demon God Form

Enter this form by using Divine Un…leashing? in Base Form with at least 2000 Sin, or by reaching maximum Sin in Goddess Form as described above.
On entering Demon God Form, Sanity is reset to 0 and switches to Enlightenment, placing Mei.L in an Enlightenment environment. When Enlightenment reaches its maximum, she returns to Base Form. If her current Health is below 15%, it is restored to 30% over 5 seconds. If her current Health is above 15%, she restores 15% Health, ignoring the maximum Health cap.
Enlightenment regenerates at 2 per second in Demon God Form.
The Nightmare Amulet and Bone Helm cannot be worn in Demon God Form. If already equipped, they are forcibly removed upon entering it.
Grants flight.
Grants night vision.

Passive Abilities:

  • Heart of the Devil: Grants 70% damage reduction as an independent multiplier, and adds 0.75 to the damage multiplier compared with Base Form.
  • Heart of Death (unlocked at Lv. 10):
    • 50% of damage dealt is treated as Planar Damage, and all damage gains 10% defense penetration.
    • Below 30% Health, each incoming hit gains an additional 80% damage reduction, and Mei.L gains Enlightenment equal to the amount prevented.
  • Heart of Blasphemy (unlocked at Lv. 20): In Demon God Form, maximum viewing range and the range at which map fog is revealed are both doubled.

Active Abilities:

  • Devil’s Blood: Doubles Enlightenment regeneration for the next 30 seconds and grants lifesteal equal to 1% of damage dealt.
  • Judgment (unlocked at Lv. 15): Grants 100 Enlightenment. For the next 30 seconds, grants +50% movement speed and immunity to slows, sleep, hitstun, and knockback.
  • A Devil’s Kindness (unlocked at Lv. 25): Available below 10 Enlightenment. Mei.L prays in place and cannot act for 5 seconds. After those 5 seconds, she immediately enters Goddess Form and restores her Health to 85%. The first enemy she personally kills within the next 15 seconds drops double loot. This effect cannot stack.
  • Game Over (unlocked at Lv. 35): Available while holding the Second Awakening item Knife · Awakened. Grants 300 Enlightenment and sweeps the surrounding area within 2 LR with tentacles, instantly killing all creatures hit, including Bosses. Cooldown: 8 minutes.

Exclusive Items and Structures

Many exclusive items can be upgraded through a First Awakening and a Second Awakening. Their name suffixes progress from · Dormant to · Unleashed to · Awakened; · Awakened therefore denotes the Second Awakening.
These upgrades use the same materials across eligible items.
To awaken an item, drop it on the ground and right-click it while carrying the required upgrade materials in your inventory.
First Awakening: Thulecite ×15, Gears ×3, Orange Gems ×3, Green Gem ×1
Second Awakening: Pure Brilliance ×20, Pure Horror ×20; requires character level 20 or higher.

Plum Blossom

Mei.L creates these by hand without a technology unlock. Crafting costs 100 Faith and 20 Sanity and produces 10 blossoms at a time. The Construction Amulet applies normally.
This recipe is available only at 0 Sin.

A food item with a 3-day shelf life. Eating one consumes it and restores 1 Sanity and 5 Health. It is also an ingredient in many exclusive items.
Regains freshness while carried in Mei.L’s built-in inventory slots, excluding backpacks and other additional slots.
Contributes a Sweetener value of 1 in a Crock Pot.
Can be used as fuel, with the same fuel value as Charcoal.
Can be used to befriend any creature that supports befriending, including Spiders. Only Mei.L can use Plum Blossoms this way.
Creatures befriended with Plum Blossoms follow for 3 times the normal duration.
Creatures befriended with Plum Blossoms are immune to fear caused by giants.
Creatures befriended with Plum Blossoms do not become hostile in response to being attacked, and instead focus more closely on the target Mei.L is attacking.

Shrine!

Crafting requirements: Alchemy Engine, Cut Stone ×10, Boards ×10, Plum Blossoms ×10, Moon Shards ×15

Only one Shrine may exist on the surface and one in the Caves: at most one per world layer.
Allows Mei.L to pray to herself and spend Faith to restore Health, Hunger, and Sanity.
While praying, Mei.L performs a prayer animation and remains stationary. Faith is consumed at 1 per second, using her own Faith first and the Shrine’s reserves second, while Health, Hunger, and Sanity each recover at 1 per second. Faith consumption does not decrease when some stats are already full. This is quite slow and mainly serves as an easy-mode assist for beginners; it is not recommended unless you prefer a relaxed playstyle.
At maximum Faith, Mei.L can deposit Faith into the Shrine. All Shrines share a storage limit of 1000 Faith. A deposit transfers all Faith currently held in one action; the amount cannot be selected. If this would exceed the storage limit, only enough Faith to fill the remaining capacity is consumed. Withdrawing likewise immediately fills Mei.L’s Faith to its maximum, or withdraws all remaining reserves if they are insufficient.
Only Mei.L herself can destroy the Shrine.
By default, actions at the Shrine that consume Faith leave 1 Faith remaining.
The Shrine also has additional upgrade features and appearances for different stages, planned for the Phase Two update.

Knife

Crafting requirements: Shadow Manipulator, Purple Gems ×3, Moon Rocks ×10, Moon Shards ×10, Meat ×10

Deals melee damage. Attack damage scales with blood-drain stacks: 20 damage at 0 stacks and 175 damage at maximum stacks.
Has infinite durability, but each use, whether hitting an attackable target or completing a tree-chopping or rock-breaking action, costs Mei.L 2 Health. Above 40 stacks, each use costs 3 Health. This Health loss can kill her.
Each time Knife consumes Health, it gains 1 stack, up to 50. If it consumes no Health during a day, it loses 5 stacks at the start of the next day.
Knife’s actions still consume Health at maximum stacks.

After the First Awakening:
Knife can perform various mining and chopping actions, with the same Health cost as attacking.
At 40 stacks or more, Health consumption is reduced to 2 per use.
Above 20 stacks, it can fell a tree or break a rock in a single action.
In Goddess Form, mining and chopping actions no longer consume Health.
In Demon God Form, its attack damage is doubled.

After the Second Awakening:
Right-click to throw Knife, dealing 1× damage at the landing point and then teleporting Mei.L to that location.
At 40 stacks or more, Health consumption is reduced to 1 per use.
In Goddess Form, holding Knife expands Mei.L’s light radius to 2 LR.
In Demon God Form, Knife gains a small area-of-effect attack, covering roughly two character body widths.

???

Crafting requirements: Alchemy Engine, Plum Blossoms ×40

Clothing with the appearance of a sailor uniform.
Wearing it prevents movement and interaction, but activates god mode, locking all stats, including Health, Hunger, Sanity, and temperature. It prevents new monster aggro but does not clear aggro acquired before equipping it, although that hardly seems to matter.
Mei.L stays in place, striking a very pitiful-looking pose.

Purge

An easy-mode assist. Decide for yourself whether to use it.

Crafting requirements: Alchemy Engine, Plum Blossoms ×100, Moon Shards ×40, Gold Nuggets ×20, Nightmare Fuel ×5, Blue Gems ×5

A Tang-style saber, named after the idea of eradicating evil completely.
Has 300 durability and grants +50% movement speed while held. Normal attacks strike with the sheath for 5 damage; its actual base damage is 30.
Normal attacks consume 1 durability; slashes consume 3. At 0 durability, the weapon remains but deals 0 damage and cannot slash. The movement speed bonus is unaffected. Each point of Faith used restores 1 durability.
Right-click and hold on a creature to charge in place. Mei.L cannot move but is immune to knockback and hitstun, and adds a mark to the target every 3 seconds. Releasing the button dashes through the target to its rear and slashes it, consuming all marks. With x marks, the slash deals physical damage equal to base damage ×3x, with final damage affected by the damage multiplier. Up to 5 marks can be stacked.
Charging is automatically canceled if the target dies. Releasing the button before the 1st mark is applied also cancels the charge. Applying a mark draws aggro as though the target had been hit once.
The slash deals 3× base damage to creatures along its path and applies Weather Pain effects, such as uprooting trees. Structures and Giant Crops receive a maximum-strength hammering effect that breaks them apart.

After the First Awakening:
Charge time per mark is reduced to 2 seconds.
In Goddess Form: Allows charging by right-clicking a structure or item. The dash slash applies the Deconstruction Staff effect to the target and to items and structures along its path.
In Demon God Form: Slashes deal true damage.

After the Second Awakening:
Actual base damage increases to 60.
Charge time per mark is reduced to 1 second.
Normal attacks deal the weapon’s actual base damage.
In Goddess Form: Mei.L cannot lose Health while charging, allowing active abilities to be used without paying their Health cost.
In Demon God Form: Grants 90% damage reduction while charging.

A Goddess Does Not Die with Empty Hands

Crafting requirements: Science Machine, Plum Blossoms ×200, Cut Reeds ×15, Orange Gems ×3

Clothing resembling a celestial feather robe.
Has infinite durability. While worn, it raises unarmed damage to 20 and unarmed attack range to 4 turf tiles.
Drains 3 Faith per second while worn.

After the First Awakening:
Grants 8 inventory slots.
Unarmed attack speed increases to match armed attack speed.
Faith drain is reduced to 1 per second.
In Goddess Form: Allows picking up items from a distance, using the same range as unarmed attacks.
In Demon God Form: Unarmed attacks apply light knockback.

After the Second Awakening:
Grants 14 inventory slots.
No longer drains Faith.
While unarmed, Mei.L can pick up heavy objects into her hand slot without suffering the carrying debuff.
In Goddess Form: Allows interacting at a distance with things such as Birdcages, Chests, and structures under construction, using the same range as unarmed attacks.
In Demon God Form: Unarmed attacks interrupt any action the target is performing and cause hitstun.

Plum Blossom Wine

Crock Pot recipe: Requires Plum Blossom and Ice, a Sweetener value of at least 2, and no Meat, Vegetables, Fruit, or Eggs. This is an alcoholic drink brewed from plum blossoms.

Drinking it applies a debuff of −2 Sanity per second for the next 30 seconds.
Stats: 0 Hunger, 15 Sanity, 0 Health.
Plum Blossom Wine has a higher recipe priority than Jellybeans.
